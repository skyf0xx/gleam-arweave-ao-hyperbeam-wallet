-------- a suggested direction:
Create a premium, minimal landing page for **GLEAM**, a modern browser-extension crypto wallet.

The site should feel like the unveiling of a **beautifully engineered physical product**, rather than a conventional crypto or SaaS website.

### Overall aesthetic

**Apple product launch × minimalist industrial design × subtle prismatic light**

Use:

- warm white / near-white background
- extremely generous whitespace
- thin matte-white product cards
- subtle physical shadows
- crisp, high-resolution GLEAM product imagery
- restrained spectral/prismatic light
- elegant typography
- almost no decorative elements

Do NOT use:

- glassmorphism
- dark cyberpunk aesthetics
- neon
- generic crypto imagery
- coins or blockchain illustrations
- excessive gradients
- feature-card grids
- phones, laptops or device mockups
- generic SaaS landing-page patterns

The rainbow should always feel like **light being refracted**, never like a colourful brand gradient.

### Page structure

There is **no navigation**.

The page consists of:

**Hero → product showcase → product showcase → product showcase → final CTA → minimal footer**

### Hero

Create a huge visual-first hero.

Small brand name:

**GLEAM**

Large headline:

**Crypto without the clutter.**

A minimal CTA:

**Get GLEAM**

The hero should be dominated by a large floating **matte-white product card containing the complete GLEAM wallet interface**.

Do not place the interface inside a device.

Make the card feel like a beautifully photographed physical product floating slightly above the white surface.

### Light

Introduce a very subtle **prismatic light source** into the scene.

The light should behave like real optical light:

- subtle lens flare
- faint spectral dispersion
- tiny rainbow refraction
- soft reflections along card edges

Never make the flare large or dramatic.

It should feel like sunlight catching a precisely manufactured object.

The same light phenomenon can subtly recur throughout the page so that it becomes part of GLEAM's visual identity.

### Product sections

Use large, spacious sections where **the product imagery does most of the communication**.

Each section should feature one large matte-white card containing one complete GLEAM interface view.

Use very little copy.

Possible sections:

**Your wallet, simplified.**

Show the clean wallet overview.

**Everything in one place.**

Show assets, activity and portfolio information.

**Built for AO.**

Show the AO / HyperBEAM experience.

**Just send. Just receive.**

Show the transaction experience.

Do not turn these into grids or collections of small feature cards.

Each section should feel like a **product photograph in an exhibition**.

### Motion

Use subtle motion to make the product feel physical.

Product cards should have an extremely slow floating movement:

- approximately 3–5px vertical movement
- 6–10 second cycles
- gentle easing
- slightly different timing between elements
- almost imperceptible

The cards should never look like they are bobbing around.

Occasionally, introduce a **very small prismatic glint** that catches an edge of a product card.

The glint should:

- happen infrequently
- move slowly
- be extremely subtle
- resemble light catching a physical surface

Do NOT use constant sweeping shimmer effects.

If possible, allow the prismatic light to respond subtly to scrolling or cursor movement, but keep the interaction almost imperceptible.

### Final CTA

End with a large amount of empty white space and one final floating GLEAM product card.

A subtle beam of prismatic light passes behind it.

Large text:

**Crypto without the clutter.**

Button:

**Get GLEAM**

### Footer

Keep the footer extremely minimal.

GLEAM

Privacy Policy · Terms & Conditions

No navigation menu or additional marketing content.

### Critical design direction

The entire page should feel like **one continuous physical environment**.

The matte-white cards, shadows, product interfaces and prismatic light should belong to the same world.

The user should feel as though they are looking at a series of beautifully photographed GLEAM objects suspended in white space.

**Whitespace, product imagery, physicality and light are the design.**

Copy should remain secondary.
