Gleam Points

Early is a position you can't buy later.

Gleam is built by its community, and everyone earns their part. The more you contribute, the more you're recognised.

What points are today

  • Holding. Every AR and AO you hold earns points, every day.
  • Inviting. Friends you bring in earn more, and so do you.

The exact rules are on the Gleam Points page.

Where they could go

One path we're exploring is a Gleam token. If there is one, points are how we'd recognise the people who were here first.

That's one possibility, not a plan we've committed to. Nothing is promised. Other places points could matter:

Partner rewards

AO projects could reward Gleam members. You'd opt in to each one and claim it yourself. We would never hand partners a list of addresses.

Fee advantages

Lower fees on services Gleam may offer later.

Premium features

Advanced features, more customisation or early access.

A say in what we build

A voice on product priorities and community projects.

Delegation

Delegating your AO yield from inside the wallet.

These are ideas, not commitments. None is announced until the design and the legal position are settled.

Why early counts

Points build from day one

A wallet starts earning the day it joins. A day you weren't here can't be added back later.

You shape it

Early members tell us what to build, and we listen. This is how the community builds Gleam.

What we won't do

  • Name a price, or a rate for turning points into anything else.
  • Share revenue with points.
  • Count down to a reward we haven't confirmed.
  • Call points ownership, or say they're worth money. They can't be bought, sold or transferred.
  • Ask you to post, follow or repost to earn. Points come from using Gleam.
  • Promise anything we haven't decided. Points are reviewed before they're used for anything, and the rules may change.

The binding wording is in the rules and our terms.
