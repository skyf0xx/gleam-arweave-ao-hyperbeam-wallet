How it works

  1. Join from your wallet

    Open Gleam and tap Gleam Points in your tokens. Joining takes one tap. If a friend sent you an invite code, paste it in.

  2. Hold AR and AO

    Once a day Gleam counts the AR and AO in each joined wallet. Every whole AR or AO earns 1 point. Fractions count too.

  3. Keep Gleam installed

    Gleam checks in quietly in the background, so there's nothing to remember. If it hasn't checked in for 3 days, that wallet earns nothing until it does.

  4. Share your invite link

    Your link is on the Points screen. Friends who join with it earn 10% extra, and you earn 10% of their points every day.

Points start the day you join

Nothing is backdated. A wallet that joins today has every day from today on its side, and one that joins next month starts from zero then.

Invites keep paying

An invite isn't a one-off bonus. You earn 10% of a friend's points every day for as long as you both keep Gleam, and the more they hold, the more you earn.

See where you stand

The Points screen shows your rank among everyone earning, updated daily. Nobody else can see yours, and there's no public leaderboard.

The rules

  • What counts. Native AR and the AO token in a joined wallet. Wrapped AR, liquidity positions and staked tokens don't. 1 AR and 1 AO earn the same.
  • When. Balances are counted once a day, around 00:00 UTC, and that day's points are added then.
  • Staying active. A wallet earns only if its Gleam install checked in during the 3 days before the count.
  • Invites. Each install's first wallet to join can use one invite code. That wallet earns 10% extra on its own points. The wallet that invited it earns 10% of those points on days it's active itself. Invite points don't earn further invite points, and there's no cap. If either wallet leaves Gleam Points, the invite bonuses between them stop, and points already earned stay.
  • Each wallet separately. Every wallet joins on its own and earns on its own balance. A wallet can leave at any time from the Points screen, which deletes its points.
  • Fair play. Points are reviewed before they're used for anything. Wallets that game the system can lose them.
  • No cash value. Points can't be bought, sold or transferred. Gleam may use them in the future, for example to decide a distribution, but nothing is promised and the rules may change. Changes are posted on this page.
