# Gleam v1.52.0 security audit

## Scope

Diff: `71dc80d..aa2d49f`. The release sets rules for motion and applies them (a collectibles swipe that follows the finger, shorter sheet timings, an undock slide, one loading look), lights the paper header, makes paper hovers catch the light, presses a disabled primary button into the paper, trims the token details screen, and brings back the header when the docked list is pulled down. It also carries #369's quiet loading and saved-site changes.

Surface paths changed, by theme:

- **Query cache.** `query-cache/retry.ts` (new) exports a 30-second retry for a read that failed and a 30-second freshness window for home reads. It sets timing only: nothing new is persisted, and the persisted cache's guards are unchanged.
- **Browser.** `BrowserView.tsx` saves and removes a site optimistically through `sites.setSaved`, sending the same `saveBrowserSite` and `removeBrowserSite` messages as before, and pops the star on a tap that saves. No origin or grant handling changed.
- **Display only.** `WalletDetailView.tsx` and `AddressDisplay.tsx` add the paper hover-glass class and keep their rest fill on hover; `RecipientCard.tsx` takes paper's hover token; `BackupSection.tsx` and `WalletDetailView.tsx` mark their chevrons so they ink in on hover; `ImportPhrase.tsx` sweeps the beam while a phrase derives instead of pulsing it. No handler, value or text a user checks changed.
- **Copy.** `i18n/messages/send.ts` drops "Your balance" and "Copied" and the arrow from "View on explorer" in every language. None of these strings appears on an approval or review screen.
- **Surface list.** `surface-exempt.txt` exempts the bundled I Was Here icon: an SVG with no script, link or embedded content.

Outside the surface: theme tokens and the paper header, the collectibles gallery and thumbnail, the home screen's docking, the token details screen, the Send and Receive buttons, the sheet primitive's timing, `DESIGN.md`. Approval sheets open in 300ms and close in 220ms like every sheet; nothing inside an approval moves.

Run: `pnpm vitest run`, tsc on all twelve projects, eslint, `pnpm deps:check`, `pnpm wxt:build`, `pnpm check:bundles`, `pnpm mobile:build`, `pnpm test:smoke`.
