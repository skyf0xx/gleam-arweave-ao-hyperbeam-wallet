# Governance

Gleam has one maintainer, who makes the final call on every change and
release.

## Roles

| Role | Who | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maintainer | [@skyf0xx](https://github.com/skyf0xx) | Sets the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md), reviews and merges changes, publishes releases, handles security reports, holds the project's accounts and keys |

## Continuity

A named successor takes over through GitHub's account successor setting.
